## Texte de la carte

> **La coquille**
>
> Votre nouveau tee-shirt porte une faute d'orthographe en gros caractères. Les captures d'écran pleuvent.

## Le moment choisi

La permanence de campagne d'Ajaccio, façade ocre et grands volets, la lumière de la mer par la porte ouverte, à l'arrivée des cartons de la nouvelle collection : un bénévole déplie le premier tee-shirt et le tend devant lui. La faute ne peut pas se lire, puisque rien n'est lisible : le tee-shirt porte de grandes lettres tracées comme des coups de pinceau sans mot, et c'est la scène qui dit la coquille, le doigt d'une bénévole posé sur un endroit du motif, le téléphone qui photographie, le fou rire qu'on retient. Le candidat reste hors champ : au premier plan, le carton ouvert et son cutter. Le bénévole regarde le spectateur avec un sourire navré. Ajaccio n'avait encore servi à aucune carte.

## Prompt complet

Dans une nouvelle conversation, avec [`primeTime.webp`](../../illustrations/media/primeTime.webp) comme seule image de référence :

```
Use the attached image as the style reference and copy its drawing: a realistic reportage drawing made on the spot for a newspaper, with quick, nervous pencil lines, often drawn twice, ink hatching in the shadows, transparent watercolour washes that spill a little over the lines, and the warm paper showing through. Not the same scene and not the same people: never reuse the face or the hair of the woman in the reference.
Like in the reference, the setting can be full when the story needs it, with the things that say where we are and what is happening, but each one is sketched with a few pencil lines and a light wash, never rendered or finished, and the drawing gets looser and paler towards the edges until it fades into the bare paper.
This must look like a drawing made by hand on paper by a press illustrator, halfway between a quick sketch and a realistic picture: never a photograph, a film poster or a digital painting, and never a cartoon, a comic strip, a caricature or a children's book illustration. Continuous lines, never dotted or dashed outlines; no uniform texture, speckles or hatching filter laid over the picture; no glossy highlights, no smooth gradients, in the sky and on the walls too. Colour can be rich in places, on a piece of clothing, a light or a flag, but it stays transparent and within the palette below. The fade into the paper at the edges stays narrow, without a wide white margin. No blur and no depth-of-field effect: everything, the foreground included, is drawn sharp, like a sketch made on the spot.
People are drawn from life with realistic anatomy and proportions, like in the reference: faces shaped with pencil lines, hatching and light washes, a little more finished than the background but never photographic (no photographic skin, no airbrushed or digital-painting look). Expressions stay natural and restrained, even in laughter or tears: no exaggerated grins, no wide-open mouths full of teeth, no rubbery or cartoonish faces.

Editorial illustration, landscape 16:9 (1536 × 864 px), the scene filling the frame, with only a light fade into the paper at the edges. The campaign's local office in Ajaccio, Corsica, in 2027, on a bright spring morning, as the boxes of the new collection arrive: ochre walls, tall shutters folded back, a tiled floor, and through the open door the Mediterranean light and the masts of the harbour. The player's candidate has opened the first box and stays off-camera: in the foreground, only the opened cardboard box full of folded T-shirts, and a box cutter on its flap. In the right third, a volunteer, a broad man in his forties, white, with curly black hair, stubble and a linen shirt, holds up the first T-shirt unfolded in front of him and looks at the viewer with a sorry, pained half-smile, his face above mid-height. The T-shirt carries a big print in large letters drawn as loose brush strokes that look like a word but cannot be read. Beside him, a volunteer in her twenties of Tunisian descent, petite, with a patterned headscarf and round glasses, points one finger at a spot in the middle of the print and holds back a fit of laughter, her other hand over her mouth. On the left, a teenage volunteer with braces and a cap worn backwards photographs the T-shirt with his phone. Only the volunteer holding the T-shirt looks at the viewer. Nothing on the T-shirt, the boxes, the phone or the walls is readable: no letter can be made out, and the mistake is shown only by the finger and the faces.

Style: Reportage drawing made on the spot, like a press illustrator or courtroom sketch artist: pencil and ink with loose watercolour washes, lively unfinished lines, white paper showing through.

Palette: navy #13203F, vellum #F5EFE1, gold #A9802A, brick #B5523B, ochre #D9A441, sage #8FA58A, slate #5E7186, sand #DCC8A4, warm grey #8C8279; use 4 to 6 of them, subtle warm paper grain.

Tone: gently ironic, a laugh held back, postures slightly exaggerated but never the faces, bright warm Mediterranean morning light.

Constraints: realistic proportions, natural expressions, never cartoonish or caricatured faces, drawn like the rest of the illustration and never photographic; people of varied ages, genders, origins and body types (heavy, stocky, round, tall, short, wiry, not only slim), each with a distinct face and haircut (no two people alike), drawn with respect and never as a joke; each person's age, build and look plausible for their job and the scene (working people of working age), no stereotype tying an origin or a gender to a role, and no person of colour shown alone in a featured service or deferential role; anatomically correct hands with exactly five fingers, few hands visible, the others in pockets, behind objects or out of frame; no readable text anywhere (the T-shirt print is brush strokes that cannot be read; the boxes, the phone and the walls stay unreadable); no captions or speech bubbles; no logos, no brand, no political party colours; no recognisable real person, no lookalike of a real politician; no explicit violence; no photographic rendering, no blur or bokeh, no dotted outlines, no texture or hatching filter over the picture, no oil-paint texture, no wide white margin or frame (a light fade into the paper at the edges is fine).

Format: if 16:9 is not available, make 1536 × 1024 and keep the top 80 px and the bottom 80 px free of anything important (faces, hands, the main action): they will be cropped.
```

## Relecture

À faire par le PO, sur l'image retenue.

- [ ] Aucun texte lisible : journaux, pancartes, enseignes, plans, tampons, écrans.
- [ ] Aucun sosie d'une personne réelle.
